# THE HOLLOW CROWN — Complete Walkthrough

## How to read this guide

**Overworld directions.** Every route starts from the Hermit's Glade, the screen where the game begins. A route like `N E2` means "go one screen north, then two screens east". Routes use the exits that actually exist, so just follow them screen by screen.

**Dungeon directions.** Every dungeon starts in its entrance room. A path like `N E N[LOCK]` means "take the north door, then the east door, then the north door". Tags on a step tell you what has to happen before you can go through that door:

- `[LOCK]` — a locked door; walk into it with a key.
- `[CLEAR]` — a shutter; defeat every enemy in the room you're standing in.
- `[PUSH]` — a shutter opened by a stone block; push the lone block in the middle of the room you're standing in.
- `(key)`, `(map)`, `(compass)` — that item is in the room you arrive in. It appears once the enemies there are defeated.
- `(dark)` — a dark room; use the Lantern to light it.
- `(hint)` — an old hermit with advice; no enemies.
- `(traps)` — spiked blocks in the corners slide toward you when you line up with them.

Every dungeon has one more key than it has locks. You can't soft-lock yourself, and keys are also sold in shops.

**The dungeon routes below are for the First Quest.** The Second Quest uses the same overworld but different dungeon layouts.

## Controls and basics

| Action | Keyboard |
|---|---|
| Move | Arrows / WASD |
| Sword | Z |
| Selected item | X |
| Inventory (pick your X item) | Enter |
| Save and quit (from the inventory) | Shift |

- **Sword beam:** at full health your sword also fires a beam.
- **Shield:** facing a pebble, spear or thrown disc blocks it, as long as you aren't swinging. The Tower Shield also blocks fireballs and magic.
- **Arrows:** the bow costs 1 gem per arrow.
- **Lantern:** one flame per screen. Leave and come back to use it again.
- **Bombs:** crack marked rock on the overworld, and open hidden walls in dungeons. Those walls look like normal wall, are shortcuts only, and are never required.

## 1. The Hermit's Glade — Twig Blade

You start in a tree-ringed glade with a small rock outcrop to the north. Walk up into the dark cave mouth in the outcrop. The hermit gives you the **Twig Blade**.

There are no enemies on this screen. Its exits are north, south, east and west.

## 2. Gift cave — a Heart Vessel

**Route:** `W4`

The cave is in the top wall. The hermit offers a choice of **Heart Vessel** or **Life Potion**. Take the **Heart Vessel**, which brings you to 4 hearts. It's one of only 16 hearts in the game, while potions can be bought anywhere.

On the way you pass the **Market Cave**, two screens west of the start (cave in the top-right). It sells the Tower Shield for 90, bombs for 20 and a key for 80. Come back for the shield once you have gems.

## 3. Easy gems

**Route:** `E2`

There's a row of three boulders near the middle of the screen. Push the middle one north by walking into it from below. Stairs appear, and the cave inside gives **10 gems**.

Defeated enemies also drop gems, hearts, bombs, fairies (heal 3 hearts) and clocks (freeze enemies).

## 4. Dungeon 1 — Rootdeep Hollow

**Overworld route:** `N E2` (temple door in the centre of the screen)

**Enemies:** slimes, flitters (bats) and bonewalkers.

**Treasure — Wind Disc:** `N(hint) E(compass) N[PUSH] N[CLEAR] W(map) N[PUSH] N(key) E`

The Wind Disc is a returning throw.
- It stuns most enemies and kills slimes and flitters outright.
- It grabs gems and hearts and carries them back to you.
- Hold a second direction while throwing to throw it diagonally.

**Boss:** from the map room (3 north of the entrance) go `N[PUSH] W[CLEAR] N[LOCK](key) N[LOCK]`. Keys are 1 room east of the entrance, in the room just north of the second push-block room, and behind the first lock.

**Boss — Thornhorn.** A horned dragon on the right side of the room. Every couple of seconds it roars and spits three fireballs in a spread. Stay at the edges of the spread, step in between volleys and slash it. It takes 6 Twig Blade hits.

Afterwards, take the **Heart Vessel** (5 hearts). Go west into the next room for the **First Dawn Shard**. Collecting a shard refills your hearts and returns you outside.

## 5. Steel Blade

**Route:** `N W4 N W2 N2 E N`

The cave is in the top wall of a mountain screen. You need **5 hearts**, so do this right after Dungeon 1. The **Steel Blade** does double damage.

## 6. Dungeon 2 — Sunscar Crypt

**Overworld route:** `N E N E N2` (desert, temple in the centre)

**Enemies:** vipers, slimes, blobs (they split into two slimes) and flitters. Vipers charge when you line up with them, so step off their line.

**Treasure — Longbow:** `N E[CLEAR](hint) E(key) N[CLEAR](key) E[CLEAR]`

**Boss:** from the entrance go `N N N[PUSH] N[LOCK] N(compass) W[LOCK]`. Both keys you need are on the treasure path.

**Boss — Coilworm.** A segmented worm that bounces around the room. Hitting any segment damages it, and it speeds up as it weakens. Stand still near a wall and slash as it passes.

The shard room is north of the boss. You now have 6 hearts.

## 7. Shopping trip

- **Lantern (60):** Lantern Shop, route `E5 N`, cave in the top wall. It also sells bombs (25) and keys (90). The Lantern lights dark rooms and burns secret bushes. **Required** for Dungeon 7 and several secrets.
- **Tower Shield (90):** Market Cave, route `W2`. Blocks fireballs and magic, which matters a lot later on.
- **Bombs:** 20 for 4 at the Market Cave or the Graveyard Shop.
- **Bomb Bag (100):** route `N W4 N W`, graveyard edge. Push the middle of three boulders north to find the Bag Seller. Capacity rises from 8 to 12.
- **Life Potions (40 each, carry up to 2):** Potion Cave, route `N E N E2`. A potion fully heals you when used from the X slot.
- **Blue Ring (250):** Graveyard Shop, route `N W4 N E N W`. It halves all damage.
  - The Secret Shop (route `E7`) sells it cheaper: burn the **3rd bush** of the row of three in the middle-right of the screen. It sells the ring for 200, the shield for 70 and keys for 60.

**Gem caves:**

| Gems | Route | How to open |
|---|---|---|
| 30 | `N W4 N W2 N2 E N2` | Bomb the crack on the right of the top wall |
| 50 | `N E N E N3 E` | Bomb the crack in the middle of the top wall |
| 100 | `N W4 N E N2 E` | Burn the 3rd of four bushes in the middle of the screen |

**Trap:** the crack at `N W4 N E N3 E2` is an angry hermit's wall. Bombing it costs you 20 gems.

**Fairy fountains** fully heal you when you stand in front of the pond: `N W3` and `N E N E N E2 N E`.

## 8. Dungeon 3 — Mirewater Vault

**Overworld route:** `E4 N2 E2` (lakeland, temple in the centre)

**Enemies:** bonewalkers, hooklings (they throw discs that come back), blobs and bulwarks. A bulwark is a shielded knight: hit it from the side or back, or bomb it.

**Treasure — Raft:** `N(key/dark) N E[PUSH](compass) E(key) N(hint) W(dark) N`

**Boss:** from the entrance go `N(key/dark) N N(map) N W[LOCK](key/traps) N N N[LOCK]`

**Boss — Bramble Hydra.** A thorny bulb with four snapping flower heads. The core can't be hurt; cut off the heads one at a time. It moves faster with each head lost, and open heads spit fireballs, so the Tower Shield helps. The shard room is east of the boss. You now have 7 hearts.

## 9. Dungeon 4 — Isle Sanctum

**Overworld route:** `N E N E N E5`

You arrive on a lake screen. The temple sits on an island. Stand on the **wooden dock** at the bottom middle of the screen and press **up**, and the Raft carries you to the island dock.

**Enemies:** flitters, vipers, blobs, hooklings and bulwarks.

**Treasure — Ladder:** `N(key) N N(traps) N W N(map) W`

**Boss:** from the entrance go `N(key) N W[LOCK](key) W(hint) N(hint) N(key) W(compass) N[LOCK]`

**Boss — Pulsar Eye.** A crab-legged giant eye that patrols the top of the room and spits fireballs. It can only be hurt while its eye is **open**. Arrows do heavy damage then, and the sword works too. Keep a few gems for arrows. The shard room is north of the boss. You now have 8 hearts.

## 10. Coastal Heart Vessels

- **Sandbar (Ladder):** route `S E`. A one-tile channel runs along the beach. Walk south across it with the Ladder to reach the sandbar, where the **Heart Vessel** sits.
- **Gull Rock (Raft):** route `E8`, the far south-east corner. Use the dock near the middle of the screen, pressing **down**, to reach the islet and its **Heart Vessel**.

You now have 10 hearts.

## 11. Dungeon 5 — Gloom Barrow

**Overworld route:** `W6` (the far west of the coast)

A one-tile water channel separates you from the barrow. Cross it with the **Ladder** anywhere that isn't directly above the temple front, for example near the left or right side. Then walk to the door from below.

**Enemies:** bulwarks, wrapped (mummies; slow but tough), hexers, blobs and bonewalkers. Hexers are sorcerers that vanish and reappear in line with you before casting. Keep moving.

**Treasure — Whistle:** `N(hint) N N(compass) W(hint) N(key) E(key) E[CLEAR](key) N N[PUSH]`

**Boss:** go through the same rooms as far as the `[CLEAR]` room, then `N W(dark) N[LOCK]`.

**Boss — Twin Coilworms.** Two purple worms at once. Fight from a corner and focus one at a time. The shard room is west of the boss. You now have 11 hearts.

**The Whistle:**
- On most overworld screens it warps you to the entrances of dungeons you've cleared, cycling through them.
- On one special screen it reveals Dungeon 6.

## 12. Dungeon 6 — Stormspire

**Overworld route:** `N E N E N3 E2` — the Mountain Tarn, a ring of cliffs around a small pool.

Play the **Whistle** anywhere on this screen. The pool drains and reveals stairs.

**Enemies:** hexers, wrapped, bulwarks, flitters and **grabbers**. A grabber is a hand that rises from the floor; if it catches you, it drags you back to the entrance. Keep moving and strike it when it surfaces.

**Treasure — Mage Wand:** `N(hint) N N[PUSH] E N[LOCK] N E(key) S(key/dark) S(key) E[LOCK](key) E`

**Boss:** follow the same path, but make the last step `S[LOCK]` instead of the final `E`.

The Mage Wand swings like a sword and fires a magic bolt every time, even when you aren't at full health.

**Boss — Many-Headed Dragon.** A dragon body at the top of the room with four heads on swaying necks. The body can't be hurt; cut down each head.
- A cut head breaks loose and flies around spitting fire until the last head falls.
- The Tower Shield helps a lot here.

The shard room is east of the boss. You now have 12 hearts.

## 13. Secret Heart Vessels and the Sunforged Blade

- **Grove Heart (Lantern):** route `N W4 N W3`. Burn the **4th** of four bushes in the middle of the screen. Stairs lead to a **Heart Vessel**. You now have 13 hearts.
- **Peak Heart (bombs):** route `N W4 N E N3 E N W`. Bomb the crack in the middle of the top wall for another **Heart Vessel**. You now have 14 hearts.
- **Sunforged Blade (12 hearts or more):** route `N E N E N E2 N E N E N`, in the north-east mountains. Bomb the crack on the right of the top wall. The **Sunforged Blade** does four times the Twig Blade's damage.

## 14. Dungeon 7 — Emberforge

**Overworld route:** `N W4 N E N3 W N` (top row of the mountains)

A row of three bushes sits on the left side of the screen. Burn the **middle bush** with the Lantern to reveal the stairs.

**Enemies:** blue hooklings, bulwarks, grabbers, vipers and blobs. Nine rooms are dark, so light them with the Lantern as you enter.

**Treasure — Spellbook:** `N(dark) E(key) S[PUSH] E(hint) E N E[PUSH](hint) S`

**Boss:** go through the same rooms as far as the second `[PUSH]` room, then `N N(map) N[LOCK](key/dark) W[LOCK] N`.

With the Spellbook, Mage Wand bolts burst into fire where they hit.

**Boss — Ember Beast.** A horned beast your sword bounces off. It hungers for bombs.
1. Drop a bomb in its path. It charges over and eats it.
2. That stuns it. Slash it while it's dazed.
3. A bomb that explodes next to it also hurts and stuns it.

If you run out of bombs, a bomb drop appears in the room after a while. The shard room is north of the boss. You now have 15 hearts.

## 15. Dungeon 8 — Shrouded Depths

**Route to the Tanglewood:** `N W4 N W2 N2`

You enter a dark, looping forest. Every exit except east just brings you back to the same screen, unless you take the right path: leave by the **north, then west, then south, then west** exits. A chime sounds when you break through. A wrong exit restarts the sequence, and the east exit always leads out of the woods.

The last west exit brings you to the temple screen.

**Enemies:** blue bulwarks, blue hexers, wrapped, flitters and blobs. Hits hurt here, so the Blue Ring and Tower Shield pay off.

**Treasure — Starlight Arrows:** `N(compass) N N[PUSH](key) E[LOCK](dark/traps) N[PUSH](key/dark) E N[CLEAR](key) N(traps) N`

**Boss:** follow the same path, but make the last step `W[LOCK]` instead of the final `N`.

**Do not skip the Starlight Arrows.** They are the only thing that can finish the final boss.

**Boss — Orbit Eye.** A great floating eye drifting around the room, ringed by eight spiked orbs. The eye can't be hurt while any orb survives. Pick the orbs off as the ring swings past you, then attack the eye. The shard room is west of the boss. You now have 16 hearts, the maximum, and all **8 Dawn Shards**.

## 16. Dungeon 9 — The Hollow Throne

**Overworld route:** `N E N W N2 E N W N` — the peak at the top centre of the map

The temple door shows a glowing seal. With all eight shards it opens; without them the seal holds.

**Enemies:** the toughest of everything, plus grabbers, traps and nine dark rooms. Bring 2 potions, full bombs and **at least 20 gems** for arrows.

**Optional — Red Ring:** `N N N[PUSH](dark) N W[CLEAR] W N W(key) N[LOCK](key/dark) N[LOCK] W(dark/traps) S S(key) S(key) W`

- The **Red Ring** cuts all damage to a quarter, and it's worth the detour.
- On the way you fight a mini **Bramble Hydra** (5 north, 2 west of the entrance) and a mini **Thornhorn** (6 north, 3 west).

**To the King:** `N N N[PUSH](dark) N W[CLEAR] W W[CLEAR](map/dark) S[LOCK]`

The `W[CLEAR]` room just before the King is dark and holds a mini **Thornhorn**. Light it, defeat the Thornhorn, then go south through the locked door.

**Final Boss — The Hollow King.** He is invisible most of the time. He flickers into view when he teleports, then fires fireballs at you.

1. **Wear him down.** Hit him with the sword, a sword beam or wand bolts whenever he appears.
2. **Wait for the stagger.** After enough hits he turns **brown** and stands dazed for several seconds.
3. **Finish him.** While he is brown, select the bow and shoot a **Starlight Arrow** into him.

If you're too slow, he recovers and you start again. Ordinary hits never kill him. Unlike other boss rooms, the doors here stay open, so you can retreat to heal.

When the Hollow King falls, the door west of his chamber opens. Walk to **Sage Elowen** to end the game.

## Ending and the Second Quest

After the ending and credits, press Enter. Your save file becomes a **Second Quest** file.

- The overworld is the same, but every dungeon has a new layout with more rooms and locks.
- Enemies have 50% more health and hit twice as hard.
- You start again with 3 hearts and no items, so use the overworld sections of this guide to re-arm.
